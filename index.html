What changed

Readable circuit structure

Power, PIC/clock/ICSP, motor/button, RF excitation, receive chain, switched antenna capacitance, and PIR interface are grouped together. Logic gates, op-amps, transistors, and passives use conventional circuit symbols. Multi-unit ICs remain on this sheet.

All symbols come from KiCad 10 libraries and were placed through the MCP server. A crosswalk maps photographed pad names to standard pin numbers; unidentified packages use explicit numbered placeholders.

Identifications and alternatives

Western candidates: MCP6002-I/SN for SGM8542; DRV8212PDSGR for a motor-driver redesign. The fitted parts retain their identities. U1/U5 await exact vendor symbols.

U1: S-1200B45 4.5 V LDO is a strong candidate: ABLIC maps PPE to this part and the fourth character to a lot code.

U2: TI SN74LVC2G14DBV is a strong candidate: C14R and the six-lead package match.

Still uncertain: Q8 is probably a dual RF switch; U6 has an LDO versus voltage-supervisor alternative.

Electrical status: The native KiCad netlist matches the authored model. This validates the file, not the physical circuit. ERC retains 80 open findings: 63 unconnected pins, 14 isolated pin labels and three undriven power checks. Numbered placeholders have passive pins and provide limited electrical checking. No continuity measurements or hardware tests were performed. PIR daughterboard internals and a physical PCB layout are outside this revision.
