Readable circuit structure

Power, PIC/clock/ICSP, motor/button, RF excitation, receive chain, switched antenna capacitance, and PIR interface are grouped together. Logic gates, op-amps, transistors, and passives use conventional circuit symbols. Multi-unit ICs remain on this sheet.

There are 20 distinct stock KiCad 10 symbol definitions and four authorized datasheet candidate symbols authored through MCP. All 352 photographed pads map to normal pin numbers. Q8 uses conventional N/P MOSFET artwork, with both units on this sheet.

Identifications and alternatives

Western candidates: MCP6002-I/SN for SGM8542; DRV8212PDSGR for a motor-driver redesign. The fitted parts retain their identities. U1/U5/U6/Q8 now have datasheet-verified candidate pin maps in the project-local library.

U1: S-1200B45 4.5 V LDO is a strong candidate: ABLIC maps PPE to this part and the fourth character to a lot code.

U2: TI SN74LVC2G14DBV is a strong candidate: C14R and the six-lead package match.

v0.7: C5=470uF/16V from user. C11 dimensions support 1206; R8 supports 0603 and the fitted resistor family is revised provisionally. Five more PIC routes were recovered in v0.7; v0.9 above supersedes that GPIO count. C32/C25/C39/R4/TP17 and ICSP protection routes corrected. Previous audit: R29/R44=18C, nominal 15 kohm. 126 stock footprints added; 147/150 assigned, with package confidence kept separate. C5/S1/LED1 remain unassigned. Latest readings: R41=331 means 330 ohm. D3=A7 supports BAV99; D4/D5=5U support SD05 TVS candidates; D6=G3 supports two Zener candidates (3.9 V or 2.4 V). D2=4P remains unidentified and its old guessed clamp connections are withdrawn.

Still uncertain: Fitted Q8 manufacturer, some copper routes and many passive values remain uncertain. The earlier RT9818 supervisor hypothesis was withdrawn after the sharper U6 photo.

Electrical status: Native KiCad 10 netlist matches all 89 modeled net partitions. This validates the file, not all board connections. ERC retains 51 findings: 37 unconnected pins, 8 isolated labels, five undriven power checks and one undriven input (LED common). No artificial power or no-connect flags hide unresolved evidence. User resistance results and selected GPIO continuity are recorded; functional testing and rail voltages remain unverified. PIR daughterboard internals and a routed PCB are outside this revision.
