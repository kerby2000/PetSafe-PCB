# Q3 readings and R33 withdrawal - v0.9.32

User measurements on 2026-10-09 were made in diode mode with Q3 fitted. Red QL / black QR read 2.3 V; the reverse read OL. Red QL / black QS initially read 0.35 V and was later remeasured as **0.48 V**. Its reverse and both QR/QS directions read OL. The later value supersedes the first for current interpretation; both are preserved in [the evidence record](../../evidence/q3_diode_results_20261009.json).

The [onsemi MMBTA55 datasheet](https://www.onsemi.com/pdf/datasheet/mmbta55lt1-d.pdf) identifies marking 2H and a PNP with base 1, emitter 2, collector 3. This supports the marking candidate, but the reported junction pattern does not confirm the drawn QR=base map. A forward 0.48 V path alone cannot distinguish a transistor junction, MOSFET body diode or an external path. No fitted-device identity or pin mapping was changed. Q3 and the untested matching devices Q4/Q5/Q6/Q11 now explicitly carry the disputed candidate qualification. Isolated testing is an optional next step, not an instruction to remove hardware without the user's choice.

The user could not find R33. The inherited inventory cited IMG_2435, which establishes R37/R38/R39 but no R33. The unsupported 10k entry and its assumed pull-up across H_PIR_RAW/H_PIR_VDD were removed, rather than renamed or marked DNP. [The withdrawal record](../../evidence/r33_withdrawal_v0932.json) preserves its original entry, two assumed net endpoints, placement and three removed wire segments. The two existing nets remain separate. Every retained physical pad keeps its previous net and native number.

This closes the R33 inventory question, not E07 as a whole: Q2 behavior and the operating PIR supply remain open. Current questions belong in [the checklist](../FINISHING_CHECKLIST.html).
