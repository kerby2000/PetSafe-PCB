## Power and references

No further resistance repeat or wiring change is warranted by these results; Q1 identity, pin roles and powered behavior remain qualified. TP5/H_RX_VDD subsequently measured 0.066 V (B62). This is consistent with absent VREF bias, but Q7 drive, supply switching and Q1 gate behavior remain unverified.

These are recorded operating-state readings, not guaranteed nominal levels.
